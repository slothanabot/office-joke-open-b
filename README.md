One single-line joke about office life, at most 200 characters, for
make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open" bounty, $2
winner-takes-all.

The joke:

> Our 15-minute quick sync had no agenda, no end time, and ended by scheduling a meeting to discuss the meeting we just had.

The angle is the "quick sync" that eats the whole day: a 15-minute meeting
with no agenda and no end, which only ends by spawning a follow-up meeting
about the meeting itself.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```

The program writes the single-line joke to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
